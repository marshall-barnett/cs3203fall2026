Best Price Book Aid

Find the cheapest place to buy your books — without buying from us.

Best Price Book Aid is a price-comparison tool built for college students and readers who want cheaper alternatives to full-price books. Unlike online retailers such as Amazon, we don't sell books ourselves — we act as a compiler of booksellers, letting you search once and instantly compare prices across multiple sellers to find the best deal available.

About

Textbooks and other required reading are expensive, and prices vary wildly between sellers. Best Price Book Aid removes the need to manually check five different bookseller sites — search for a title once, and we aggregate pricing across sellers so you can see the best available price in one place.

Features
Search for a book by title, author, or ISBN
Compare prices across multiple booksellers in one view
Surface the lowest available price for a given book
No checkout or fulfillment through us — you buy directly from the seller with the best price
